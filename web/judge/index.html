The model makes the turn decision.

02 / Inside StutterTurn

We build on Pipecat Smart Turn v3.2. Our work is the adaptation, evaluation, and replay system around a concrete question: is this person finished, or still speaking through a pause?

  1. 01

    Listen to the current turn

    16 kHz mono audio. Silero VAD detects speech and silence; both replay configurations use a 0.2 s silence gate.

  2. 02

    Represent the sound

    Up to eight seconds of audio becomes an 80 × 800 feature input using Whisper’s feature extractor.

  3. 03

    Score whether the turn is complete

    Stock FP32 or our adapted X1 artifact runs through ONNX Runtime on the Vultr backend.

  4. 04

    Hold, or prepare a reply

    The score is compared with the configuration’s threshold. A complete decision requests a response; resumed speech can cancel it.

Two different jobs

When to answer.
Then what to say.

The turn model decides when to request a reply. Gemini generates what to say, and ElevenLabs voices it.

A turn decision, a generated reply, and scheduled playback are separate events. The replay records each stage; sound reaching the listener is not measured by these timestamps.

03 / Training & selection

W3 training sampling mix

80/20

80% original-model replay
20% stutter-focused examples, split between unfinished and completed examples.

Training sampling proportions, not a train/test split.

Fine-tune → blend → export → replay

W3 fine-tuned reconstructed stock FP32 weights. X1 uses a weight blend: 75% of the W3 epoch-one checkpoint and 25% stock, then an ONNX export and a 0.60 decision threshold.

The replay compares that complete configuration with Stock FP32 at 0.50. It can show different behavior; it cannot attribute the difference to training alone.

Exploratory X1: post-hoc candidate selected after v4 results; fifth adaptive look at dev/guard, not a v4 pass. Human confirmation remains pending and the reserved final test stays sealed.

How to evaluate it

Use the live workspace for runs. Use the recorded artifacts to check what the evidence supports.

Open live workspace
  1. 01

    Keep the recording fixed

    Sign in to the workspace and wait for automatic connection, then choose a recording. Use Retry connection if loading fails. Keep the same source for every comparison. These are recorded inputs; this path does not capture a live microphone or phone call.

  2. 02

    Compare one configuration at a time

    Start with Stock (FP32), then use a fresh run for our X1 candidate on the longer source above. Then compare Stock (INT8) with Patient: the silence gate changes from 0.2 s to 1.0 s on the same model. The candidate was not included in the historical five-clip replay and is not a promoted model.

  3. 03

    Separate a decision from a response

    Listen to the source and reply, then find the turn-complete decision, resumed caller speech, and first reported agent audio. A reply can be cancelled before it plays. The event record separates these moments; server-scheduled times are not measured speaker output.

  4. 04

    Check the artifacts and the limits

    Match the run’s source, configuration, and model metadata to the logs. Treat mock or development preview events as synthetic. The historical five-clip replay uses three-second excerpts followed by five seconds of synthetic silence; those clips cannot establish completed-turn response delay.
