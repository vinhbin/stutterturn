The live demo now runs on Vultr and connects automatically after sign-in. Choose a recording and configuration, then Start to hear the caller and generated reply. Volume, Stop, and Reset control playback.

Gemini generates replies; ElevenLabs streams the speech to your browser. Tiger Data queries power the judge guide’s saved event chart. New cloud runs are not automatically ingested into that chart.

The adapted candidate is exploratory, not promoted. The earlier replay beside this is an engineering snapshot; human-reviewed confirmation and response-delay evaluation remain ahead.
